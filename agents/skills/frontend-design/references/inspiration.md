# Frontend Inspiration

Use these as references for visual direction, not templates to copy.

- [Anton Skvortsov](https://dribbble.com/AntonSKV) — polished product and web concepts with strong typography, motion, and visual hierarchy.
- [Canvas UI](https://canvasui.dev/) — source-available, copy-in components for selective WebGL and HTML-in-canvas effects across React, Solid, Preact, Vue, Svelte, and vanilla TypeScript. Consider it for expressive brand surfaces, not routine product UI. Verify accessibility, performance, reduced motion, and graceful degradation: its HTML-in-canvas path currently requires an experimental Chrome capability, while WebGL effects have broader support. The MIT + Commons Clause license permits use in applications but restricts reselling or redistributing the components themselves.
