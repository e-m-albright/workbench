## Recommendation

Renew for one year while requiring a reliability plan before the next renewal. Usage increased, but the proposed price and two unresolved incidents prevent a stronger commitment.

| Evidence | Current period | Comparison | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Active usage | 134 units | 100 units | Up 34% year over year |
| Proposed price | $111,000 | $100,000 | Up 11% |
| Reliability incidents | 2 unresolved | 0 target | Requires an operating plan |

## Decision conditions

1. Name an owner and dated corrective plan for both reliability incidents.
2. Hold the term to one year while the operating evidence remains incomplete.
3. Review adoption and incident closure before the next renewal window.

## Caveats

The comparison does not establish that usage growth caused proportional business value. It also does not estimate migration cost or the effect of negotiating different commercial terms.

<details>
<summary>Method and assumptions</summary>

The example uses synthetic annualized values. “Active usage” has one consistent definition across both periods. Proposed price excludes implementation services and taxes.

</details>
